Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CCS Ventas Cars LLC (“CCS Ventas Cars,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects information you share with us through this website, by phone, by text message and in person. CCS Ventas Cars LLC is located at 1477 W Landstreet Rd Ste B, Orlando, FL 32824.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us, including:

  • Your name, phone number and email address;
  • Vehicle details, such as the car you are interested in, your down-payment range, and the year, make, model and mileage of a vehicle you want to trade in;
  • Your preferred language (English or Spanish);
  • Your text-message consent choices, including which boxes you checked and when you submitted the form.

When you submit a form, we also record the date and time, the page you submitted it from, your IP address and your browser type, so we can keep an accurate record of your request and your consent choices. Our website forms do not ask for your Social Security number, date of birth or income. The full credit application is hosted by our dealer-management platform and is covered by its own privacy terms.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests;
  • To follow up about financing and trade-ins you ask us about;
  • To send appointment, application and service messages related to your request;
  • To send marketing messages, only if you agreed to receive them;
  • To keep records required by law and to protect against fraud and misuse of our website.

Who processes your information

We share your information only with service providers that help us run our business and respond to you, such as our customer relationship management (CRM) and communications providers, our website hosting provider and our dealer-management platform. These providers may use your information only to provide services to us. If you apply for financing, your application is shared with the lenders you are applying with. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS / Text Messaging

SMS / Text Messaging. If you opt in to receive text messages from CCS Ventas Cars LLC, we will use your mobile number to send the types of messages you agreed to. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use Google Tag Manager and related analytics tools, which use cookies and similar technologies to help us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and which buttons are tapped. The site also loads fonts and an embedded map from Google. These services may receive your IP address and browser information under Google’s own privacy terms. We store your language choice in your browser so the site remembers it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to your request, complete a sale, provide follow-up service and meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations. Records of your text-message consent and opt-outs are kept for as long as needed to show we honored your choices.

Your choices: access and deletion

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing ccsventascars@gmail.com or calling (786) 548-8238. We may need to verify your identity first, and we may keep information we are legally required to retain. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
