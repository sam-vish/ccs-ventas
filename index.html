05Warranty

Drive off covered.

We offer GWC Warranty plans, from basic coverage of a vehicle's most expensive components to comprehensive plans that rival a manufacturer's factory warranty.

Warranty details ↗
Most protection

Exclusionary Coverage

Covers so much it's easier to list what it doesn't. Everything the manufacturer's basic factory warranty would cover is included, except the parts listed under "Exclusions From Coverage" in your contract.

Targeted protection

Stated Component Coverage

Covers the parts and systems listed on your contract. If a covered component fails, the plan pays for parts and labor so you're back on the road quickly.

Coverage, terms and eligibility vary by vehicle and plan. Ask us which plans fit the car you like. Ask about coverage

08Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí. You can ask questions, review paperwork and talk through financing and warranties in Spanish or English.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, we offer financing options. Send the short form above and we'll call you, or go straight to our online credit application.

Can I trade in my current car?

Yes. Send us your car's year, make, model and mileage through the trade-in form. The final value is confirmed after we see the vehicle in person.

Do your cars come with a warranty?

We offer GWC Warranty plans you can add to eligible vehicles. Coverage and terms depend on the vehicle and the plan, so ask us which ones apply to the car you like.

What if I can't find the car I want?

Use Car Finder. Tell us the make, model and budget you're after, and we'll let you know when we find a match.

Are you open on Sunday?

We're closed Sundays. You can still send a financing or trade-in request any time, and we'll call you back on our next business day.

How do I find you?

We're at 1477 W Landstreet Rd, Suite B, Orlando, FL 32824. The building has several dealers, so look for Suite B. Tap "Directions" to open the map.
